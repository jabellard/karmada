## What's a serviced apartment?

A **serviced apartment** is a fully furnished rental that combines apartment living with hotel-style services. It's the alternative to a hotel in this plan.

**What you get**
- **Full apartment**: separate bedroom, living room, and a real kitchen — not just a hotel room.
- **Hotel services**: housekeeping, reception/front desk, sometimes a gym/pool, often on-site laundry.
- **Longer-stay friendly**: billed by night but designed for weeks/months, so rates drop vs. a hotel over 14 days.

**Why it fits this trip**
- **Remote work week**: a desk, kitchen, and more space beat a cramped hotel room for 5 days of calls.
- **Dating**: a private, nice apartment is more comfortable for hosting than a hotel-lobby vibe.
- **Rest**: cook when you want, spread out, feel like you live there instead of visiting.

**Bottom line**: the middle ground between an Airbnb (space, kitchen, privacy) and a hotel (reliable service, front desk, generator/fiber). In Santo Domingo, look for these in Piantini — ask a concierge or search "serviced apartment Piantini" alongside the JW Marriott option.

## Do Airbnbs provide hotel-style services (housekeeping, etc.)?

Generally **no** — the key difference from a serviced apartment or hotel.

- **One cleaning** between guests (the "cleaning fee"); no daily housekeeping, front desk, room service, or linen swaps. Self-service.
- For a **14-day stay** this matters. Options:
  - **Ask the host** for mid-stay cleaning (extra fee — common for longer bookings).
  - **Hire your own** — housekeeping is cheap in Santo Domingo.
  - **Serviced apartment or hotel** — services built in, zero arranging.
- Given the "stress-free" priority, this favors a **serviced apartment / JW Marriott** over a plain Airbnb. If Airbnb, confirm mid-stay cleaning up front.

## Bringing guests to my room (dating + privacy)

The original plan didn't address this — it's a real gap.

- Many **DR hotels enforce a guest policy**: visitors may need to **register + leave a government ID** at reception; sometimes overnight-visitor restrictions. Upscale chains (JW Marriott, Intercontinental) tend to be **stricter**. Handing over ID at a front desk can be a dealbreaker on a casual date.
- **Best for privacy**: a **serviced apartment or Airbnb with a private entrance** — your own door, no front desk logging guests. Apart-hotels/condo buildings (just a security guard) are also discreet.
- If choosing a hotel, **ask the guest policy before booking**: "Can I have adult guests visit my room? Is registration required?"
- **Net effect**: tips the balance toward a serviced apartment or private-entrance Airbnb in Piantini. Confirm up front: (1) free guest policy, no registration; (2) fiber + generator.
- Reflected in `plan.md` §1 (Guests / privacy) and §7 (dating).

## Is Uber Eats used widely there?

It exists in Santo Domingo but **isn't the leader** for food delivery.

- **PedidosYa** — dominant delivery app in the DR (absorbed Glovo). Widest selection, most reliable. Install first.
- **Uber Eats** — present but thinner coverage/selection; fine as a backup since you'll have an Uber account.
- **WhatsApp** — many restaurants take delivery orders directly; normal in the DR.
- **Rides**: plain **Uber** is excellent and widely used (the one that matters most — already in the plan).
- **Payment**: add a card, but keep pesos on hand (some orders/drivers prefer cash).
- **Piantini** has huge delivery coverage (sushi, Italian, steak, local) — great for WFH lunch days.
- Reflected in `plan.md` §6 (Food) and the checklist.
