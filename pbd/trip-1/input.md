# Trip Planning Request

## About me
- Software engineer, traveling solo.
- Based in NYC; flying out of JFK.
- First time in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic.

## The trip
- Two weeks off, staying in Santo Domingo the whole time (single base).
- Goal: a great, rejuvenating trip. Rest and recharge first.
- Working remotely one of the two weeks; chilling the other.
- Money is not a constraint. I'll pay for a great, stress-free experience.

## What I want
- Mostly chill, but easy access to activities when I feel like them.
- Lots of food options nearby.
- To meet and date local Dominican women.
- To never feel limited in my choices.

## What the plan must cover
1. **Where to stay** — best neighborhood in Santo Domingo, and why.
2. **Booking** — how to book flights and accommodation, with specific recommendations.
3. **Remote work** — reliable internet, workspace, and setup. My team depends on me that week.
4. **Logistics** — door to door: leaving my house, airport, arrival, getting around Santo Domingo.
5. **Relaxing** — how to actually rest and recharge in a city.
6. **Food** — where and what to eat, lots of variety.
7. **Dating** — realistic, respectful ways to meet local women.
8. **Activities** — optional things to do, bookable on short notice.

## Rules
- Be comprehensive but concise. If it fits in 10 words, don't use 20.
- Give specific, actionable recommendations, not generic advice.
- Optimize every step for stress-free and easy.
