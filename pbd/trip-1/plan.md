# Dominican Republic — 14-Day Plan

## The strategy
One base the whole time: **Santo Domingo**. No mid-trip moves.
- **Week 1 (work):** keep your team's hours, explore evenings.
- **Week 2 (chill):** recharge, date, optional day trips.

Why one base: zero friction, best internet, deepest food and dating scene.
Beaches are optional day trips, not the point.

Why not a split or resort: adds packing/transfers, and resorts isolate you from locals.

---

## 1. Where to stay

**Neighborhood: Piantini or Naco** (upscale, safe, walkable).
- Everything nearby: restaurants, cafés, bars, gyms, rooftops.
- Zona Colonial (historic, nightlife) is a 15-min Uber away.

**Property**
- Serviced apartment or **JW Marriott / Intercontinental Real** in Piantini.
- Must-haves: fiber internet, desk, backup generator (power cuts happen), pool, gym.
- Airbnb in Piantini/Naco works; filter "Guest favorite" + Superhost; confirm generator + fiber in chat.
- Book refundable.

**Guests / privacy (important for dating)**
- Prefer a **serviced apartment or Airbnb with a private entrance** — no front desk logging visitors.
- DR hotels (incl. JW Marriott / Intercontinental) often require guests to **register + leave ID** at reception — awkward on a casual date.
- Confirm before booking: "Can I have adult guests visit, no registration required?"

---

## 2. Booking

**Flights (from JFK)**
- **JFK → SDQ** nonstop (~4 hrs), both ways. Delta, JetBlue, American daily.
- Book on Google Flights; buy direct on the airline site.
- Fly JetBlue Mint or Delta One for the rested arrival.
- Book now; prices rise inside 3 weeks.

**Everything else**
- Pre-book airport transfers both ways.
- Book lodging refundable.

---

## 3. Working remotely (week 1)

- Base yourself where the fiber is; test speed on arrival (target 50+ Mbps up).
- Backup #1: local eSIM (**Claro** or **Altice**) via Airalo, bought before you fly.
- Backup #2: coworking day pass — **WK Coworking** or **Impact Hub**.
- DR is EST year-round (no DST); US-East overlap is clean.
- Tell your team your setup and backups before you leave. Set status honestly.
- Work mornings, explore evenings. Protect your standup and key meetings.

---

## 4. How to relax when you're not working

You're in a city, so relaxation is curated, not automatic. Build a rhythm:

**Daily anchors**
- Slow mornings: balcony coffee, no screens for the first hour.
- Hotel pool or gym midday; sauna/spa if available.
- Evening walk on the **Malecón** at sunset.

**Weekly resets**
- Book a **spa day/massage** (hotels and standalone spas are cheap and excellent).
- One beach day trip: **Juan Dolio** (~45 min) or **Boca Chica** — private driver, no stress.
- A quiet café afternoon in Zona Colonial with a book.

**Mindset**
- Don't over-schedule. One planned thing per day, max.
- Say yes to spontaneous plans; leave the rest open.
- Silence notifications outside work hours.

The goal: structure enough to unwind, loose enough to never feel limited.

## 5. Logistics — door to door

**Departure (JFK)**
- Carry-on only if you can; check nothing.
- Bring: laptop, chargers, power bank, passport. No adapter (DR uses US plugs).
- Leave for JFK ~3.5 hrs before takeoff; car service, not subway/AirTrain.

**Arrival at SDQ**
- Buy the **e-Ticket** (migration form) online 72 hrs before each flight — required.
- Pre-book a private transfer or use a hotel car; skip taxi haggling.
- Get pesos from an airport ATM; cards work most places.

**Getting around**
- **Uber** everywhere in the city; cheap and reliable.
- Private driver for day trips; your hotel arranges it.

## 6. Food

- Local: **La Cocina de Doña Marina**, **Adrian Tropical** (mofongo).
- Upscale: **Mesón de Bari**, **SBG**, **Buche Perico**.
- International: Piantini has sushi, Italian, steak — everything.
- Casual/street: empanadas, chimi burgers, fresh fruit juices.
- Try: mofongo, sancocho, tostones, passion fruit juice, Presidente beer.
- Delivery: **PedidosYa** (dominant; install first), Uber Eats (backup). Many spots also deliver via WhatsApp. Huge coverage in Piantini — great for WFH lunches.

## 7. Meeting local women (respectful + realistic)

- Apps: **Tinder** and **Bumble** are widely used; set location to Santo Domingo.
- In person: rooftop bars in Piantini, Zona Colonial cafés, salsa/bachata socials.
- Learn basic Spanish; even a little goes a long way. Use it.
- Be genuine, take her somewhere nice, be a gentleman. Respect > game.
- Safety: meet in public, watch your drink, keep valuables secure.
- Privacy: pick lodging that lets you host freely (private entrance, no guest registration) — see §1.

---

## 8. Optional activities (bookable same-day)

- Walk **Zona Colonial** (oldest city in the Americas).
- **Los Tres Ojos** caves; Malecón sunset drive.
- Bachata/salsa class.
- Beach day trip: Juan Dolio or Boca Chica.
- Overnight escape if the mood hits: Samaná or Punta Cana.

Book via your hotel concierge. No advance planning needed.
